Where you'll stay

A family home

Bangli matches you with one of the host families in the kampung — a private room in a real family home. You'll eat with them, share the common spaces, and experience the village from the inside.

The families here are part of Malaysia's official Homestay Programme — registered, trusted, and genuinely welcoming.

  • Private room
  • Home-cooked meals included
  • Activities arranged through Bangli
  • Families matched to your group size
  • English-speaking coordination

The community

Life on Pulau Tuba

The island has its own rhythm — fishing at dawn, meals shared at long tables, music in the evenings. You're not visiting a tourist attraction. You're staying with the people who live here.

Bangli

Coordinator, guide & lifelong resident of Tuba

Bangli manages the whole operation — host family matching, tours, transport, and whatever else comes up. He speaks English, knows every corner of the island, and has been welcoming visitors for years.

Just tell him your dates and how many people. He'll take care of the rest.

Before you come

Getting here & what to know

Getting here
Ferry from Kuah Jetty, Langkawi · around 15 minutes · runs daily
Best season
High season October–May · June to September brings rain and rougher seas
What to bring
Light clothes, sun protection, cash — there is an ATM on the island
Pricing
Built around your group and dates · ask Bangli for a package
Languages
Bahasa Melayu on the island · Bangli speaks English
Who is this for
Families, friend groups, solo travellers — anyone who wants the real thing
